# BloodLink AI - User Manual

## 1. Getting started
1. Install the app and open it. Swipe through the 3 intro screens or tap Skip.
2. Choose your role on the Sign In screen: **Donor**, **Requester** or **Blood Bank**.
3. New user? Tap "Sign up", fill the form, accept Terms & Privacy, and verify your phone with the 6-digit SMS code.
4. Allow **location** and **notifications** when asked. Both are needed for matching and alerts.

## 2. Donors
- **Home:** shows your blood type, eligibility status, urgent appeals nearby and your next eligible date. Use the "Available to donate" switch to stop or start receiving alerts.
- **Check eligibility:** Donate tab, answer the questions, tap "Check Eligibility". You see Eligible / Not eligible with reasons and the date you can donate again.
- **Respond to an alert:** tap the notification, review hospital and units, then Accept or Decline. After accepting, the requester sees your ETA. Go to the hospital/bank and show your app.
- **After donating:** the blood bank verifies your donation. It appears in History and your reminder date updates.
- **Camps and banks:** open the map to find nearby blood banks and donation camps, then RSVP.
- Tip: if you cannot come after accepting, cancel quickly. Repeated no-shows lower your reliability score.

## 3. Requesters
1. Tap **New Blood Request**.
2. Enter patient name, hospital and ward, blood type, units, urgency and contact number. Pin the hospital location.
3. Tap **Find AI-Matched Donors**. Donors are ranked by compatibility, distance and reliability and notified automatically.
4. Open **Request Tracking** to watch live status: Created, Notified, Accepted, Arrived, Fulfilled. Contact the donor once they accept.
5. If nobody responds, the search area widens automatically. For life-threatening emergencies also call **1990** (Suwa Seriya) or the hospital blood bank.

## 4. Blood banks / hospitals
1. Register with your NBTS licence number and official details. Your account is **Pending** until an admin verifies it; you will be notified.
2. **Dashboard:** stock per blood type, low-stock warnings, incoming donors, 7-day AI forecast.
3. **Inventory:** record received, used, expired or adjusted units. Every change is logged.
4. **Incoming donors:** verify a donor on arrival, mark donation completed or rejected.
5. **Camps:** create a camp with venue, date and target units; donors nearby are informed.

## 5. Admins
Sign in via Admin Login. Review pending bank applications (approve or reject with a reason), watch fraud/no-show flags, suspend accounts, view regional analytics and send awareness alerts.

## 6. Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No SMS code | Check number format (+94...), wait 60 s, tap Resend |
| No alerts | Enable notifications, keep "Available to donate" on, allow location |
| Map empty | Turn on GPS and internet |
| Account pending | Banks must be approved by an admin (usually within hours) |
| Not eligible | Read the reasons; most are time-based and clear automatically |

## 7. Privacy and safety
Your exact location is never shown to requesters before you accept. You can request data export or deletion from Profile. Eligibility guidance does not replace medical screening.
